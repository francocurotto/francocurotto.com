I'm still using my 2015 tablet and I love it 😍

2026-XX-XX

Every day, I use my outdated Lenovo Yoga Tab 3 8.0, YT3-850F, released on October 2015 (bought on 2017 for about $100 USD) for reading, watching videos and more, and I don't have plans on updating it. Here I plan to showcase the benefits and limitations of daily driving ancient Android hardware.

PHOTO

What I love 😍

  • On the hardware side: this tablet includes a kickstand that allows you to put the screen almost vertically. This is ideal to watch videos while my hands are busy: like when I wash the dishes, fold my cloth, or doing anything in the bathroom. To my knowledge, there is no modern tablet that includes this kind of kickstand. The closest one is the newer version of the same tablet, the Lenovo Yoga Tab, from 2021, and even that kickstand one seems like weaker version of the original version. I know that there exists cases that for modern tablets that allow to set the tablet in vertical position, but in my opinion nothing beats the stability and ease to use of a builtin stand.
    • PHOTO of stand
  • In order to implement the stand, the tablet present a cylindrical protuberance along one of its long edges. Although this seems like an inconvenience, it is was used spectacularly well by the designers. It holds a big 6200 mAh battery. It also hold a huge stereo audio system and a big power button. My favorite one, a camera that can be spin around the cylinder to turn in to a standard and a selfie camera!
    • PHOTO of protuberance
  • Even more so, the non-flat shape and shift in center of mass that the protuberance produces make it very easy to handle with one hand, even when walking casually.
    • PHOTO of holding
  • On the software side: the OS is old (Android Marshmallow 6.0.1 (released on December 2015). Yet these of version of Android were very lenient in which apps can be disabled (I can disable Google Play Store, Chrome, and even Google keyboard). Also these version didn't have so much uninstallable bloat, and without updates I'm glad I'll don't have to deal with Google horrendous Developer Verification Update (although it still affects all due to its impacts on developers).
  • The mere fact of using a +10 year old computing hardware without major issues is cool enough!

What I don't mind 🙂, or I had to fix 🛠️

  • Even though hardware this old can show it age, I really don't mind it for the things I use. For example its 0.88GB of RAM is more than enough for reading and video.
  • Its 16GB of storage(with almost 5GB taken by System) is a little bit more limiting, but it is workable by using a by SD for external storage.
  • While many apps are no longer compatible with the system, there more than enough apps for the things I do, and even if the last version of an app is no longer compatible, I can get a previous version from APKmirror(see List of apps for details).
  • Still there is no compatible web browser that is modern and can browse the web with ease in such old hardware. This is no problem as I hardly need to visit a website for the things I do in the tablet. For those rare occasion I need to see a site, I use a old but compatible browser.
  • Around 2020, the tablet start to behave sluggishly. In order to lower the burden on the OS I had to uninstall and disable every app that I didn't use. I even deleted Play Services and all Google related apps. It worked wonderfully, and the tablets behaves as good as new.
