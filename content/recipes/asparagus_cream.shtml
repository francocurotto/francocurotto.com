🌱 Asparagus Cream in Thermomix

📋 Ingredients

  • 🌱 250g asparagus
  • 🧅 75g onion
  • 🫒 15g olive oil
  • 🥣 250g vegetable bouillon
  • 🥛 50g milk
  • 🧂 1/4 tsp salt

🔢 Instructions

  1. Add onions and cut at 5s//s5.
  2. Add olive oli and fry at 5m/120C/s1.
  3. Remove asparagus ends, and cut 3/4cm pieces.
  4. Add asparagus and cook at 10m/100C/s1.
  5. Wait 2 minutes.
  6. Crush at 1m//s5→s10.
  7. Add milk and cook at 2m/80C/s2.

💡 Ideas

  • Replace cream for milk for more creamness.
  • Add 75g of 🥔 potato for milk for more thickness.
  • Add pepper for more flavor.
