🥕 Carrot Soup for Two in Thermomix

📋 Ingredients

  • 🥕 300g carrot in slices
  • 🧅 50g onions
  • 🧄 1 clove of garlic
  • 🫒 15g olive oil
  • 🥗 350g vegetable broth
  • 🥛 50g milk cream

🔢 Instructions

  1. Chop the onions and garlic at 5s//s5.
  2. Add the olive oil and fry at 5m/120C/s1.
  3. Add carrots and chop at 5s//s5.
  4. Add the broth and cook at 20m/100C/s1.
  5. Add the cream and crush at 1m//s5→s10.

💡 Ideas

  • 🧂 add salt and/or pepper to your liking.
