🍦 Matcha Ice Cream in Thermomix

📋 Ingredients

  • 🥛 500g milk
  • 🍦 200g cream
  • 🍬 120g sugar
  • 🍳 3 egg yolks
  • 🍵 2tsp matcha powder
  • 🧂 1 pinch salt

🔢 Instructions

  1. Add milk, cream, sugar yolks, and salt and cook at 8m/90C/s3.
  2. Add matcha with a sieve and mix at 20s//s4.
  3. Cold in fridge for 4 hours.
  4. Move to container and freeze in freezer for 8 hours.
  5. Cut in pieces and crush at 30s//s6, then 30s//s10.
